About us

We build applications where AI does real work.

RamanujTech is a technology studio focused on AI-based application development — platforms that read, reason over, and surface signal from information most tools skip past. Kautilya, our first product, reads geopolitical reporting across languages rather than relying on English-language wire coverage alone.

It's the first of several products on our roadmap, each built the same way: narrow, verifiable claims over broad, unverifiable ones — and shipped to a real audience, not left as a demo.
